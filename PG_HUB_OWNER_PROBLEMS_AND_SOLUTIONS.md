# PG Hub: The Everyday Problems PG Owners Can Run Better

## The owner reality

Running a PG is not just about filling rooms. Every day, owners have to answer rent questions, check payments, follow up with tenants, remember empty beds, manage electricity charges, handle deposits, and keep records ready. When these tasks live in WhatsApp chats, notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory, the owner becomes the bottleneck.

PG Hub brings those recurring operations into one Android-first workspace, so owners can spend less time chasing updates and more time running a healthy property.

## Problems today — and what PG Hub makes easier

| What PG owners struggle with | What usually happens today | What PG Hub helps them do | Result for the owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chasing monthly rent** | The owner checks messages, calls tenants, and tries to remember who has paid. Partial payments are especially easy to miss. | Record UPI, cash, and bank payments; see paid, partial, pending, and overdue status in one rent view. | A clear payment picture without manual checking. |
| **Sending awkward payment follow-ups** | Each reminder is typed individually and important details such as amount or due date are missed. | Create context-aware WhatsApp reminders for a tenant, or send bulk reminders for outstanding rent. | Faster, more consistent follow-up with less friction. |
| **Giving receipts after payment** | Receipts are handwritten, delayed, or not available when a tenant needs proof. | Generate a professional digital receipt after recording a payment and share or save it. | Better tenant trust and a dependable record of every collection. |
| **Not knowing which beds are vacant** | Room occupancy is tracked informally, so empty beds and available capacity are unclear. | Manage rooms, bed capacity, and tenant assignments; see vacant rooms and available beds in reports. | Quicker action on vacancies and better occupancy decisions. |
| **Keeping tenant details and movement organised** | Join dates, rent, due dates, phone numbers, room shifts, and move-outs are spread across notes or chat history. | Add tenants to rooms, retain their payment history when shifting rooms, and mark a tenant as left. | One reliable tenant record instead of repeated data entry. |
| **Calculating move-out dues and deposit refunds** | Final bills are manually calculated; pro-rata rent or deductions can create disputes. | Calculate pro-rated rent, utility deductions, deposit settlements, and final refunds during move-out. | Clearer settlements and fewer arguments at exit. |
| **Splitting electricity and AC charges fairly** | Bills are divided manually, which is slow and difficult to explain. | Track utility billing and split electricity, AC, and custom charges by room, person, or meter. | More transparent charges and less billing confusion. |
| **Losing track of daily expenses** | Collections are known, but operating costs are tracked separately or forgotten. | Keep expenses and monthly budgets alongside rent collections. | A more realistic picture of property performance. |
| **Managing short-stay guests** | Day guests are mixed with regular tenants or not recorded consistently. | Track day guests by room with custom rates and see their revenue on the dashboard. | Short-stay income is visible rather than incidental. |
| **Preparing reports or reconciling payments** | The owner builds spreadsheets at month-end and compares multiple payment sources manually. | Review collection, revenue, occupancy, payment mode, tenant movement, pending-rent, and reconciliation information; export the monthly rent sheet to Excel. | Faster month-end review and more confident decisions. |
| **Managing more than one property** | Each PG has separate records, and switching between them causes blind spots. | Switch between multiple properties from one owner workspace. | A single operating view as the business grows. |
| **Knowing what needs attention today** | The owner reacts to interruptions rather than prioritising the day. | Use PayFlow AI to ask practical questions such as who still owes rent, what changed, or which reminders should go out. | Clear next actions instead of information overload. |

## A simple monthly workflow with PG Hub

1. Set up the property, rooms, beds, and tenants once.
2. At the start of the month, review the rent sheet for paid, partial, pending, and overdue amounts.
3. Send individual or bulk WhatsApp reminders where needed.
4. Record incoming payments and share digital receipts immediately.
5. Track utility charges, expenses, day guests, and room movement as they happen.
6. At month-end, review collections, occupancy, payment methods, vacant beds, and tenant movement; export the rent sheet if required.
7. When a tenant leaves, complete the move-out settlement with pro-rated rent, deductions, and deposit refund details.

## The value PG Hub creates

PG Hub does not replace the owner’s judgment. It removes repetitive follow-ups, scattered records, and avoidable calculation work so the owner can operate with clarity.
